# Thumbnails: what the data says (researched 2026-11)

No public study proves what drives CTR: every big dataset is correlational. The only causal tool
is YouTube **Test & Compare** on your own channel. [U] = unverified/vendor.

## Rules
1. **The idea carries the click; the face adds emotion.** Faces are on ~84% of breakouts but also
   ~82% of flops (Eden, 2,960 thumbs); with/without face ~equal on 300k outliers, faces *hurt* in
   Business, help in Finance (1of10). Keep the face, never rely on it.
2. **Three elements max:** face + 2-4 words + one story sticker (a group of words = one element).
   Readable in under 1 s at ~160 px wide (MrBeast team: 10-20 s to decode = scrolled past).
3. **Expression: real, readable, mouth closed.** Neutral/serious slightly over-represented in
   breakouts (51% vs 47%); shock is rare and not better; MrBeast's team: closed mouth won 30 of 30
   tests. For a business/tech creator: concerned/skeptical, determined (closed mouth), wry half-smile.
4. **Gaze:** eye contact when the face is the story; look/point at the sticker when the object is
   the story (eye-tracking on ads: averted gaze moves attention to the product). Good first A/B.
5. **Words: 2-4, never the title** (only 6% of breakouts repeat the title). White/accent text with
   outline or box (68% white, 73% outlined on covers that have text).
6. **Colour/brightness: no measurable effect** (Eden pixel analysis). Stand out from the feed,
   avoid near-black/near-white edges that merge into YouTube's UI, avoid YouTube red/white/black.
7. **Keep the bottom-right corner clear** (duration stamp).
8. **Series consistency:** same font, accent (#FFD23F), relight side, and the chip
   ("SERIES NAME | EP N") every episode (MrBeast: face + consistent format since 2019).
9. **Specs:** 1280x720, JPG < 2 MB. In Test & Compare, a variant under 720p downscales all to 480p.

## Packaging
- Title + thumbnail are written together as one story; the thumbnail tells what the title doesn't.
- Title under ~60 characters, hook word first, most extreme *true* framing, never overpromise
  (MrBeast guide: mismatch -> "lied to" -> click off).
- **Test & Compare**: up to 3 variants, judged on **watch-time share** (not CTR), max 2 weeks,
  desktop Studio only, don't edit mid-test. Make the 3 *very* different (layout/concept first,
  expression/colour later); expect "Inconclusive" at low views. Small channels can simply swap the
  packaging in week 1 if a video underperforms; watch CTR in the first 48 h.

## Photo, not a video frame
Pros shoot the base image separately (Vy Qwaint, MrBeast's team). Video frames = motion blur,
compression, mid-word mouths. **2-minute shot list** (rear camera 2x/3x lens, 1-1.5 m, tripod,
self-timer/burst; same shirt as the video; window light 45° on the side the script relights; plain
background; exposure locked on the face; ~10 burst frames per pose):
1. neutral/serious, eye contact, mouth closed · 2. determined smirk · 3. skeptical (brow down,
head tilt) · 4. looking + pointing toward the text side · 5. holding the phone/laptop to camera ·
6. repeat 1 and 4 facing the other way (never mirror the face).
At 30 cm a phone makes the nose ~30% wider than at 1.5 m (Rutgers/Stanford 2018): stand back.

## Face polish
- Restoration models rebuild faces from a prior: identity drift and plastic skin are the known
  failures. No evidence top creators use them; pros fix the source + light retouch (dodge/burn,
  brighten eyes, masked smoothing, rim light, blurred/saturated background).
